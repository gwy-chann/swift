Motorcycle Parts & Repair Shop Management System

An integrated Point-of-Sale (POS), warehouse inventory tracker, and motorcycle repair bay management platform tailored for high-velocity shop floor and workshop operations.

1. Brand Identity Overview

Core naming conventions, position statements, and operational scope.

Brand Name SWIFT (stylized in bold uppercase with expanded tracking)
Official Tagline Motorcycle Parts & Repair Shop Management System
Extended Descriptor Integrated POS, Inventory & Motorcycle Repair Bay Management System
Supported Color Modes Light Mode (Clean Slate & Steel Blue) + Dark Mode (Deep Obsidian & Vivid Blue)
Target Portals Admin Portal (Analytics, Pricing, Users) & Staff Portal (Fast-lane POS, Shelf Locator, Punch Clock)

2. Light Mode vs. Dark Mode Architecture

SWIFT is architected with dual-theme design tokens defined in globals.css:

☀️ Light Mode: "Clean Slate & Steel Blue"

Default interface optimized for well-lit retail cashier counters and inventory barcode scanning desks.

  • Canvas: Soft Cool Slate (#f1f5f9)
  • Cards: Pure White (#ffffff)
  • Primary: Steel Blue (#2563eb)
  • Sidebar: Deep Slate Navy (#0f172a)
🌙 Dark Mode: "Deep Obsidian & Vivid Blue"

Tailored for motorcycle repair bay grease-pits, mechanical workshops, and reduced glare.

  • Canvas: Deep Obsidian (#0b0f19)
  • Cards: Charcoal Slate (#151f32)
  • Primary: Vivid Sky/Neon Blue (#3b82f6)
  • Sidebar: Pitch Black Navy (#070b14)

4. Sub-Brands & Modules

Identifiable feature brands integrated into the SWIFT ecosystem.

Compatibility Engine

Smart MotoMatcher

Motorcycle fitment matrix matching models (Yamaha, Honda, Suzuki) with OEM standard and performance aftermarket replacement parts.

Service Bay

Swift Workshop

Repair bay service tracking and flat-rate labor services (CVT cleaning, oil change, tire mounting, electrical repair).

Checkout

Fast-Lane POS

Rapid cashier terminal supporting dual retail/wholesale pricing, discounts, and printable receipt generation.

Warehouse

Rack & Shelf Locator

Physical warehouse mapping format (e.g. Rack A-01 / Shelf 2) for rapid item retrieval and fulfillment.
